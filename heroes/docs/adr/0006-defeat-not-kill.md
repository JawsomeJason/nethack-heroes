# 0006 Defeat, not kill

## Status

Accepted, 2026-11-11

## Context

The theme is superheroes.
Stock wording and rules around killing fit it badly.

## Decision

Enemies are "defeated".
Human enemies leave no corpse.
Most early enemies are machines.
Stock rules that fit the theme badly stay in for now.
These are eating corpses for powers, altar sacrifice and genocide.

## Consequences

* The wording change is about two strings of C.
* No corpse for human enemies is a flag per monster.
* Corpse-eating powers still work on non-human enemies.
* The "You murderer!" penalty and similar rules are not reworked.
* Reworking the stock rules needs a new ADR.

## Evidence

Untested: all of it. No prototype was built for this decision.
The scope of the C change is an estimate from reading the source.
